# Code of Conduct

Blackhatbadshah is a technical engineering community. Keep discussions factual, constructive, and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be technically rigorous and respectful.
- Challenge ideas with evidence, not people.
- Give credit to prior work.
- Keep discussions relevant to the issue or change.
- Protect private, confidential, and security-sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks
- deliberate misinformation presented as technical fact
- publishing credentials, private information, or confidential material
- malicious disruption of project infrastructure or discussions

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its contributors.
