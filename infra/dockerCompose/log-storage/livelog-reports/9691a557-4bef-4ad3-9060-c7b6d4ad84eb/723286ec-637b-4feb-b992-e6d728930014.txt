## 6. REMEDIATION PLAN

### **Issue: Rsyslog Suspension**
- **Immediate Actions**:
  - Check disk space and I/O status: `df -h`, `iostat`.
  - Restart `rsyslog` service to stabilize temporarily: `systemctl restart rsyslog`.

- **Short-term Fix**:
  - Review rsyslog configuration for action queues and file write settings.
  - Increase queue size or switch to `disk-assisted` queues to buffer logs during transient issues.

- **Long-term Solution**:
  - Implement centralized logging (e.g., ELK or rsyslog + Kafka) to reduce local disk I/O.
  - Monitor rsyslog queue metrics and alert on repeated suspensions.

- **Code/Config Changes**:
  ```conf
  $ActionQueueType LinkedList
  $ActionQueueFileName srvrfwd
  $ActionResumeRetryCount -1
  $ActionQueueSaveOnShutdown on
  ```

- **Monitoring Recommendations**:
  - Alert on `rsyslogd: action ... suspended` in logs.
  - Monitor disk I/O wait time and free inodes.
