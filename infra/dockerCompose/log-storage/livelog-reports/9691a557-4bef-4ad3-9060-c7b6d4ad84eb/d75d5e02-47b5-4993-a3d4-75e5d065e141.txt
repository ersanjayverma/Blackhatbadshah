# Log Analysis Report

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The logs cover a 2-hour period from **2026-01-19 21:07:10** to **2026-01-19 21:17:12** and reveal a server under active **brute-force SSH attack** from multiple IP addresses. The system shows good defensive measures with **UFW firewall blocks** and **fail2ban** implementation, but there are **critical Rsyslog service interruptions** that could impact security monitoring. **14 failed SSH login attempts** were recorded from various IPs targeting multiple invalid users, while legitimate services like Dovecot and Postfix function normally.

  (This pattern repeats multiple times throughout the logs for both `action-0` and `action-5`)
- **Impact**: **Critical** - Security logs may be lost during suspension periods, creating blind spots in attack monitoring and forensic analysis.
