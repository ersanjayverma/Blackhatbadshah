## 7. PREVENTIVE MEASURES
1. **Centralised Session Auditing** – enable `auditd` or `systemd` `LoginWatchdogSec` to capture detailed info (PID, command line) for every login event.  
2. **Limit Session Frequency** – use PAM `pam_limits.so` to set `maxlogins` or `login.max_per_user` to prevent runaway scripts.  
3. **Health‑check Dashboard** – integrate `systemd-logind` metrics (session count, start/stop events) into Grafana/Prometheus for visual trend analysis.  
4. **Process‑level Logging** – ensure services that may spawn login shells (cron, CI agents) also emit their own logs, making correlation trivial.  
5. **Test Reboot Scenarios** – run regular reboot validation tests to confirm `systemd-logind` returns to a clean state without residual sessions.
